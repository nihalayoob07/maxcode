NVIDIA API key

  1. Open NVIDIA's key page and sign in (free).
  2. Click Generate API Key and copy it.
  3. Paste it here.
Your key stays on this PC, encrypted, and is only sent to NVIDIA.

Courses

Options

Max hides under the bottom of the screen with his hand up for 2 minutes, then out of sight. Click the hand, or press Ctrl+Alt+M any time, to call him; he hides again after 5 minutes idle.

He dresses for the time: pyjamas at night, sleepy with coffee early in the morning, sneaking bites of a sandwich at lunch, a cap at weekends, and hats for Halloween, Christmas and New Year. He jumps when a bug gets fixed.
